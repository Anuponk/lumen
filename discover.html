How to play

  1. Look at the grid. Each color marks a territory.
  2. Place the Guardians. Put exactly one in every row, column and territory. Two Guardians can never touch, even diagonally.
  3. Light up your sky. Every solved puzzle lights a star in a real constellation.

Play at your own pace. Every puzzle has one solution you can find by logic. Read the full rules and a worked example.

The real sky, one constellation at a time

Lumen features all 88 official constellations, from Andromeda to Ursa Minor, drawn from real star positions. Play 880 quests across 9 Adventures, from the first glimmer to the complete sky.

Earn badges by playing quickly, without assistance or without mistakes. Reach 100% Mastery in an Adventure to unlock the next one for free.

No ads

Lumen is made by a small independent studio. There are no ads, and Lumen does not collect data for ad targeting. The first Adventure (100 quests) is free. You can unlock further Adventures through Mastery or with a small purchase that supports hosting and development.
