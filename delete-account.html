Delete my Lumen account

Version française · Español

You can request deletion of your account and associated Lumen data here without reinstalling the app or signing in again.

Nopuna — Cédric Valente EI handles the request. Your authority to make the request must be verified before any deletion. Never send a password, sign-in code or full backup.

What this request covers

We will review your Lumen profile, progress, game data, challenges, notifications and associated account data when handling the request. We will explain any data that must be retained and the relevant reason.

This request applies only to data associated with Lumen. Data used by other services is outside its scope.

A child's legal representative can contact us to exercise the child's rights. You can leave out the child's date of birth when using this form.

Your email app opens with a message for you to send. This page prepares the message. Sending it and processing the request are separate steps.

You can also write directly to valente.cedric@gmail.com, including if you no longer have access to the account email address.

What happens next?

We will reply to verify the request and confirm when it has been processed. Preparing a message does not send it or delete the account.
