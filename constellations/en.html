The 88 constellations of the sky

Every constellation has a name and a story. Explore the 88 official constellations and the stories behind them, then find them again in Lumen.

Constellations from A to Z

A constellation is a region of the sky as seen from Earth. Its area is measured in square degrees, and its stars can lie at very different distances.

88 constellations

Andromeda

Where does the name come from?
This constellation is named for a princess in Greek mythology. Chained up and threatened by a sea monster, she is rescued by Perseus.
Did you know?
Under a very dark sky, the Andromeda Galaxy can be seen with the unaided eye as a small, faint patch.
