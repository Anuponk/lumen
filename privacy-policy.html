Who handles your data?

Lumen is published by Nopuna — Cédric Valente EI, the data controller. Contact us at valente.cedric@gmail.com. Business contact details are in the legal notice.

What Lumen uses

  • Without an account: your progress, attempt and settings on your device, plus the caches and pending operations required to run the game.
  • With an account: your email or sign-in provider identity, account ID, nickname, progress, performance, access rights and the receipts needed to restore your game and prevent duplicate operations.
  • Leaderboards: nickname, rank and best time including penalties for each puzzle.
  • Challenges: nickname, challenge ID and results relevant to the participants. Voluntary feedback: your message and its context. Notifications: your browser subscription and preferences if you enable notifications.
  • Reliability: limited diagnostics about version, platform and sync, including the state of Lumen’s local space on the device (rounded size, usage share and write-failure type: storage full or unavailable), without any content or error message. These help track errors and serve no backup function.
  • Purchases: see the “Purchases” section below.

Optional statistics are off by default. Signing in does not give consent to statistics. Optional incident details require separate consent, which you can withdraw from your account. A withdrawal requested offline is retried when you reconnect. Temporary support requires additional specific consent. It shares filtered copies of progress and diagnostics in a private space accessible to authorised support staff, with an access log. It does not replace your saves, and withdrawing consent does not delete them.

Why do we use data and on what legal basis?

  • Performance of a contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)): your account, sign-in, saving and syncing your game, purchases and access to purchased adventures.
  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)): notifications, optional statistics, optional incident details and temporary support. You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting the rest of the game.
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)): pseudonymous leaderboards, challenges, limited reliability diagnostics, service security and aggregated measurement of the shop and daily limits (shop opened, offer tapped, payment started, cancelled or failed, limit reached and message shown; never any payment data), for signed-in accounts only.
  • Legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)): keeping sales records for accounting.

Leaderboards and opting out

Eligible performances from accounts with a unique nickname appear on leaderboards. For each puzzle, signed-in players can see only your nickname, rank and best time including penalties. Email addresses and account IDs stay private. “Echoes” marked “Virtual” are game targets. You can opt out from your account. Your public times are removed, your choice is saved and you keep your progress. Only an explicit decision to rejoin reverses your opt-out. Private attempt records support the 15-minute cooldown and prevent duplicates until your Lumen data is erased. Older records do not carry over to the leaderboards.

Who can receive the data?

The publisher and people it authorises, as well as the providers needed to operate the service:

  • o2switch (France): hosts these pages.
  • Supabase: sign-in, database and server processing. The database is in the European region (eu-central-1). Supabase describes support and operational processing outside that region, including in Singapore and the United States, under standard contractual clauses. See transfer information.
  • Resend: transactional emails. Resend stores message data in the United States. Transfers are covered as described in its GDPR information.
  • Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., Ireland): payments on the web version. Stripe may transfer data to the United States under the safeguards described in its privacy policy.
  • Google: payments in the Android app (Google Play), and sign-in if you choose “Continue with Google”.
  • Your browser's notification services, if you enable notifications.

Purchases

Lumen has no advertising. LUMEN Premium is an optional one-time purchase. See the terms of sale. Google Play handles payment in the Android app and Stripe handles payment on the web. You enter your payment details and email address on the provider's page. Lumen never receives your card details.

For each purchase, Lumen keeps the provider, purchase or checkout session reference, product, amount, currency, status (paid, refunded or disputed), status history and dates. For web payments Lumen also records when you requested immediate access and waived the right of withdrawal. Lumen uses these data to carry out the sale, grant purchased access, handle refunds and disputes, and meet accounting obligations.

Purchase data linked to your account is deleted along with it. A minimal accounting record with no account ID, email, nickname or progress is then kept until 1 January of the eleventh year after purchase (Article L123-22 of the French Commercial Code). Google and Stripe retain their own payment data under their respective obligations.

How long do we keep data?

Your account, progress, badges, records, access rights and receipts needed for sync are kept for as long as your account exists. A warning is planned after 24 months of inactivity. Deletion may be initiated after 30 months without signing in. Automatic deletion of inactive accounts is currently disabled.

Applied or planned periods for other data: confirmed technical sync observations, 30 days. Optional detailed diagnostics, seven days. Support cases, six days after activation, and their logs, seven days. Challenges, 12 months after their last activity. Resolved technical diagnostics, 30 days after resolution. Closed feedback, six months. Optional statistics, 90 days. Shop and daily-limit measurements, 90 days. Disabled notification subscriptions, 90 days. Security logs, six months. Evidence of handling data requests, 12 months after closure. Some data is purged by a daily job that can be delayed by an outage. The publisher removes other data. Provider logs and backups follow those providers' retention periods.

Exercise your rights

Under the conditions set by the GDPR, you can write to the publisher to request access, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability, or to object to processing. We may need to verify your identity proportionately. Never send passwords, sign-in codes or full backups.

We respond within one month. For complex requests we can extend this by up to two months. We tell you within the first month if we need an extension and give the reason. You can also complain to the CNIL.

Request deletion

The account deletion request page prepares an email for you to send. It performs no erasure or automatic sending. The publisher handles the request after you send it.

Deletion applies to data associated with Lumen. Data from other services remains outside its scope. Deletion removes your identity from challenges while preserving other participants. People who already received a challenge may have kept a copy. To prevent old saves from restoring deleted data, we keep a pseudonymous record of the type, hashed identifiers and date of erasure. Copies on your devices and provider backups follow their own lifecycle. There is no guarantee of immediate erasure from those copies.

Choices and children

Guest play is available without an account. Notifications follow your choice and your device permissions. Lumen does not offer supervised child accounts. A minor may purchase only with permission from a parent or legal representative. A legal representative can contact the publisher to exercise a child's rights, subject to proportionate verification.
